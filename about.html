Ahmed Abdel Gawad

Financial Planning & Analysis Leader — MBA, CMA (US)

Ahmed Abdel Gawad is a finance professional with over 10 years of progressive experience, providing critical support for executive-level decisions across various industries.

Throughout his career, he has served in analytical roles covering Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A), Reporting, Treasury, and Control functions. His expertise in introducing reporting enhancements has significantly improved transparency and focus on key business drivers.

Ahmed brings a strong blend of hands-on experience across a broad range of finance functions, coupled with persuasive communication and an interest in leveraging data analytics for strategic decision-making.

He has a demonstrated ability to analyze complex business cases, identify risks and opportunities, and translate financial insights into actionable recommendations that drive business performance.
